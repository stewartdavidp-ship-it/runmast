Works with what you already use

Replace your stack.
Or sit on top of it.

Most small businesses sell on more than one channel — and that's where things break. A product description gets updated in one place but not the other. Stock sells out on Etsy while Shopify keeps taking orders. The customer who bought at the fair never makes it into your newsletter list.

Mast is the source of truth that keeps every channel in sync. Edit a product once, update everywhere. One inventory, one customer list, one set of numbers — no matter how many storefronts you run.

A
Replace mode — go all-in on Mast
Drop your Shopify/Square/Etsy subscriptions. Run sales, site, marketing, and books on Mast. One bill, one login, fewer moving parts.
B
Side-by-side mode — keep your existing channels
Keep selling on Shopify, Etsy, Square, or anywhere else. Mast sits behind them as the one product, inventory, and customer system — pushing updates out, pulling orders in.
C
Hybrid mode — pick and choose
Sell on your Mast storefront and Etsy. Take fair sales on Mast POS while keeping Shopify for wholesale. Mast doesn't care — your inventory still adds up.
